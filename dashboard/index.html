Today, all standard online security (Banking, Government, Passwords) relies on RSA and ECC encryption.

Quantum computers using Shor's algorithm will easily break RSA/ECC. Hackers and hostile states are harvesting encrypted data today and storing it until quantum computers can unlock it.

Key takeaway: We must detect cyber-attacks in milliseconds and upgrade to quantum-resistant encryption before large quantum computers arrive.

QI-CTD converts network packet clues into quantum physics math to catch attacks in 3.8 milliseconds without needing expensive quantum computers:
